Fuel Density

How do vegetation and climate shape a wildfire?

A forest is also fuel.

Trees and shrubs can fuel a wildfire. Suppressing fires allows more vegetation to accumulate. Warmer, drier conditions make that vegetation more likely to ignite. Change the conditions, start a fire, and observe what remains.
